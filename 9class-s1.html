Working Model of Automatic Railway Gate

Back

Objective

  • To design and demonstrate a working model of an automatic railway gate.
  • To understand how automation improves railway safety.
  • To reduce accidents and human effort at railway crossings.

Need for the Project

Many railway accidents occur due to human errors or delay in closing railway gates. Some crossings do not have gatekeepers, which increases the risk of accidents.

  • Human error while operating gates
  • Delay in closing gates
  • Unmanned railway crossings

This automatic railway gate system helps improve safety by:

  • Closing the gate automatically when a train arrives
  • Opening the gate after the train passes
  • Reducing the need for manual gate operation

Materials Required

  • Cardboard or thermocol base
  • Toy train or handmade train model
  • DC motor or servo motor
  • IR sensors (2) or switch sensors
  • Battery (9V or AA)
  • Wires
  • Gate rods (ice-cream sticks or straws)
  • Switch
  • LED lights (optional)
  • Glue, tape, cutter
  • Arduino board (optional)

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Prepare the Base – Fix a cardboard or thermocol sheet and draw railway tracks and road crossing.
  2. Create Railway Tracks – Use black paper or sticks to create railway tracks and place the toy train.
  3. Install the Gate – Make gate barriers using ice-cream sticks and connect them to a motor.
  4. Fix the Sensors – Place one sensor before the crossing and another after the crossing.
  5. Electrical Connections – Connect sensors to the motor using wires and battery or Arduino.
  6. Testing – Move the train toward the sensor and observe the automatic gate movement.

How It Works

  • When the train approaches the crossing, the first sensor detects it.
  • The sensor sends a signal to the motor.
  • The motor automatically closes the railway gate.
  • After the train crosses the gate, the second sensor detects the train leaving.
  • The motor receives another signal and opens the gate again.

This system uses automation and electricity to control the gate without human operation.

Result / Output

  • The railway gate closes automatically when the train approaches.
  • The gate opens automatically after the train passes.
  • The model successfully demonstrates accident prevention using automation.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how automation technology can improve railway safety. An automatic railway gate system helps prevent accidents, reduces human error, and ensures safer transportation for both vehicles and trains.
