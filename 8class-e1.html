Carbon Footprint of My Daily Life

Back

Introduction

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, mainly carbon dioxide (CO₂), released into the atmosphere due to human activities.

In our daily life, we indirectly produce carbon emissions through transportation, electricity use, food habits, waste generation, and the use of products made from fossil fuels.

Understanding our carbon footprint helps us identify activities that produce emissions and encourages us to adopt eco-friendly habits.

Objective

  • To analyze how daily activities contribute to carbon emissions.
  • To understand the concept of carbon footprint.
  • To study the environmental impact of greenhouse gases.
  • To learn ways to reduce personal carbon emissions.

Concepts Used

  • Carbon Footprint
  • Greenhouse Effect
  • Global Warming
  • Fossil Fuels
  • Sustainable Development
  • Climate Change

Major Sources of Daily Carbon Footprint

Transportation

  • Vehicles running on petrol or diesel release carbon dioxide.
  • Private vehicles produce more emissions than public transport.

Electricity Consumption

  • Electricity is often produced from coal and fossil fuels.
  • Using fans, air conditioners, lights, refrigerators, and gadgets increases emissions.

Food Habits

  • Food waste produces methane gas in landfills.
  • Some foods, especially those from livestock farming, have higher carbon footprints.

Plastic and Consumer Products

  • Production and disposal of plastic release greenhouse gases.

Cooking Fuel

  • Fuels such as LPG release carbon dioxide when burned.

Environmental Impact

  • Global warming
  • Melting glaciers
  • Rising sea levels
  • Irregular rainfall
  • Heat waves
  • Extreme weather events

Carbon dioxide traps heat in the atmosphere and increases the Earth’s temperature.

Materials Required

  • Large chart paper or cardboard base
  • Sketch pens or colors
  • Printed images of daily activities
  • Glue and scissors
  • Cotton (for smoke effect)
  • Green paper for eco-friendly section
  • Small labels

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Draw the Earth in the center of the chart and write the title “Carbon Footprint of My Daily Life”.
  2. Surround the Earth with drawings of daily emission sources such as a car with smoke, AC or fan, plastic bag, gas stove, and dustbin.
  3. Use arrows pointing toward the Earth and label them as CO₂ emissions.
  4. Create another section showing solutions such as bicycle use, solar panels, tree planting, and public transport.
  5. Add a comparison table showing high carbon activities and low carbon activities.

How It Works

Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas that traps heat in the Earth's atmosphere through the greenhouse effect. When large amounts of CO₂ are released from human activities, the Earth's temperature rises.

  • Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases.
  • These gases trap heat and increase global temperature.
  • Reducing fossil fuel use helps lower carbon emissions.
  • Eco-friendly habits help protect the environment.

Expected Result

  • The model explains the sources of daily carbon emissions.
  • Students understand the environmental impact of human activities.
  • The project encourages sustainable lifestyle choices.

Conclusion

Our daily activities contribute to carbon emissions, but small lifestyle changes can significantly reduce our carbon footprint.

Using public transport, saving electricity, reducing waste, and planting trees are simple steps that help protect the environment and reduce climate change.
