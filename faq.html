Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Your questions about Student Project Hub answered here

Home

1) What is Student Project Hub?

Student Project Hub is an educational website that provides Science and EVS project ideas for students from Class 5 to Class 10.

2) Who can use this website?

Students from Class 5 to 10, teachers, and parents can use this website for project ideas and guidance.

3) What subjects are covered?

  • Science
  • Environmental Studies (EVS)

4) Are the projects syllabus-based?

Yes, all projects are designed according to school syllabus concepts and are suitable for academic submissions.

5) Does the website provide project material list?

  • Aim
  • Materials Required
  • Procedure
  • Working Explanation
  • Conclusion

6) Are these projects easy to make at home?

Yes, most projects use simple and easily available materials that students can find at home.

7) Is this website free to use?

Yes, students can explore project ideas and basic content for free.

8) Can teachers use these projects for classroom demonstrations?

Yes, teachers can use the project ideas for classroom teaching and exhibitions.

9) Do you provide step-by-step guidance?

Yes, every project includes clear step-by-step instructions for better understanding.

10) Can students submit their own projects?

Yes, students can share their innovative project ideas through the submission section.

11) How do I choose a project?

You can select your class (5th–10th) and then choose from Science or EVS project categories.

12) Are working models included?

  • Hydraulic Arm
  • Rain Alarm
  • Electric Circuits
  • Water Filtration System

13) Can I download project content?

Some resources may be available for download in PDF or document format.

14) Does this website help in science exhibitions?

Yes, the projects are suitable for school exhibitions and science fairs.

15) Is registration required?

Registration is optional, but creating an account helps you save projects and access extra features.

16) How can I contact support?

You can contact us through the Contact Us page for any help or guidance related to projects.
