Plastic Pollution in My Area

Back

Introduction

Plastic is widely used in our daily life for bags, bottles, packaging, and containers. However, plastic does not decompose easily and remains in the environment for hundreds of years.

In many areas, plastic waste can be seen on roadsides, near shops, in drains, and sometimes in open spaces. This causes serious environmental pollution and affects the health of humans and animals.

This project helps students study the problem of plastic pollution in their local area and understand its harmful effects on the environment.

Objective

  • To study the problem of plastic pollution in the neighbourhood.
  • To understand the harmful effects of plastic waste.
  • To learn ways to reduce plastic use.
  • To spread awareness about keeping the environment clean.

Concepts Used

  • Plastic Pollution
  • Non-Biodegradable Waste
  • Environmental Protection
  • Waste Management (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)
  • Sustainable Living

Materials Required

  • Chart paper or cardboard
  • Colors or sketch pens
  • Pictures of plastic waste (optional)
  • Glue and scissors
  • Small plastic pieces for model (clean waste only)
  • Green paper for trees and clean surroundings

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Divide the chart or model into sections such as causes, effects, and solutions.
  2. Draw or create a polluted area showing plastic bags on roads and clogged drains.
  3. Show the harmful effects like animals eating plastic or dirty surroundings.
  4. On another side, draw a clean environment with cloth bags, recycling symbols, and dustbins.
  5. Add a slogan such as “Say No to Plastic, Save Our Planet”.

How It Works

Plastic is a non-biodegradable material, which means it cannot be broken down by microorganisms. Because of this, plastic waste remains in the soil and water for many years.

  • Plastic bags and bottles pollute land and water.
  • Animals may eat plastic waste and become sick.
  • Plastic blocks drainage systems and causes waterlogging.
  • Burning plastic releases toxic gases and increases air pollution.

Survey Observation

  • Plastic bags are commonly used in local shops.
  • Dustbins are sometimes overflowing.
  • Some people throw plastic waste on roads.
  • Only a few people use cloth or reusable bags.

Expected Result

  • The project shows the causes and effects of plastic pollution.
  • Students understand the importance of reducing plastic waste.
  • The project encourages people to use eco-friendly alternatives.

Conclusion

Plastic pollution is a serious environmental problem in many areas. Improper disposal of plastic waste pollutes land, water, and air.

Using cloth bags, recycling plastic, avoiding single-use plastics, and spreading awareness can help reduce plastic pollution and keep our surroundings clean and healthy.
