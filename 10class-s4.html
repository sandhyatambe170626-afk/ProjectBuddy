Study of Electroplating on Different Metals

Back

Aim

To study the process of electroplating by coating different metal objects with a thin layer of another metal using electric current.

Introduction

Electroplating is the process of depositing a thin layer of one metal over another metal using electric current. It improves the appearance of objects, prevents corrosion, and increases durability. This experiment helps us understand basic concepts of electrochemistry and corrosion prevention.

Principle

Electroplating works on the principle of electrolysis. When electric current is passed through an electrolyte solution, metal ions from the anode get deposited on the cathode (object to be plated).

Materials Required

  • Copper sulphate solution (CuSO₄)
  • Copper plate (Anode)
  • Iron nail / steel spoon / metal key (Cathode)
  • DC power supply or 3–6V battery
  • Connecting wires with clips
  • Beaker or plastic container
  • Sandpaper
  • Distilled water

Setup Diagram (Label in Model)

  • Battery
  • Copper plate (Anode +)
  • Iron object (Cathode –)
  • Copper sulphate solution

Procedure / Steps

  1. Clean the iron object using sandpaper to remove rust and grease.
  2. Pour copper sulphate solution into a beaker.
  3. Connect the copper plate to the positive terminal of the battery.
  4. Connect the iron object to the negative terminal of the battery.
  5. Immerse both metals into the solution without touching each other.
  6. Switch on the power supply and allow current to pass for 10–15 minutes.
  7. Remove the iron object and rinse it with water.

Observation

  • A thin reddish-brown layer of copper is deposited on the iron object.
  • The copper plate slowly dissolves into the solution.

Result

The iron object gets coated with a thin layer of copper. This confirms that electroplating occurs due to the movement of metal ions under the influence of electric current.

Conclusion

Electroplating successfully coats one metal with another, improving its appearance and protecting it from rust and corrosion.

Applications of Electroplating

  • Gold plating on jewelry
  • Chromium plating on car parts
  • Tin plating on food cans
  • Silver plating on utensils

Advantages

  • Prevents corrosion
  • Improves appearance
  • Increases durability
  • Economical use of costly metals

Precautions

  • Ensure proper battery connections
  • Do not touch the solution with bare hands
  • Clean the object properly before plating
  • Use low voltage only
