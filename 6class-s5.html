Water Purification Using Natural Filters

Back

Introduction

Clean water is very important for drinking and for good health. However, water from rivers, ponds, or rain may contain dirt and impurities.

Water can be cleaned using different filtering methods. Some natural materials like sand, gravel, charcoal, and cotton can help remove dirt from water.

In this project, we make a simple water filter using natural materials to understand how water purification works.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate how natural materials can be used to filter and clean dirty water.

Materials Required

  • Plastic bottle
  • Cotton or cloth
  • Sand
  • Gravel or small stones
  • Charcoal pieces
  • Dirty water
  • Cutter or scissors (use with adult help)
  • Container or glass

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Cut the plastic bottle into two halves.
  2. Turn the top half upside down to make a funnel shape.
  3. Place cotton or cloth at the bottom of the bottle.
  4. Add a layer of charcoal above the cotton.
  5. Add a layer of sand above the charcoal.
  6. Add gravel or small stones on the top layer.
  7. Pour dirty water slowly into the bottle.
  8. Collect the filtered water in a container placed below.

Observation

The dirty water becomes clearer after passing through the natural filter layers.

Result

The natural materials remove dirt and impurities from the water.

Conclusion

Natural filters like sand, charcoal, and gravel can help clean dirty water and make it clearer.
