Introduction

Sound is a normal part of our daily life, but too much sound can become noise pollution. Noise pollution is caused by loud sounds from vehicles, factories, loudspeakers, and construction work.

Excessive noise can affect human health by causing stress, hearing problems, and difficulty in concentration. A noise pollution indicator is a device that helps detect high levels of sound and gives a signal or warning.

In this project, we create a simple model that shows how noise levels can be detected using basic electronic components.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate how high levels of sound (noise pollution) can be detected using a simple indicator.

Materials Required

  • Sound sensor module or microphone sensor
  • LED lights
  • Buzzer
  • Battery
  • Connecting wires
  • Breadboard or cardboard base
  • Switch (optional)

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Connect the sound sensor module to the battery using wires.
  2. Attach LED lights and a buzzer to the sensor output.
  3. Fix all the components on a cardboard base.
  4. Turn on the circuit using the switch.
  5. Create sound near the sensor by clapping or speaking loudly.
  6. Observe how the LED light or buzzer reacts to the sound.

Observation

When the sound level increases, the LED lights glow or the buzzer starts to sound, indicating high noise levels.

Result

The device successfully detects loud sounds and indicates noise pollution through light or sound signals.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how a simple electronic device can help detect and indicate high noise levels in the environment. Such systems can be useful in monitoring noise pollution in cities and public places.
