Comparative Study of Natural vs Chemical Fertilizers

Back

Aim

To compare the effect of natural fertilizer and chemical fertilizer on plant growth.

Concepts Involved

  • Plant nutrients (NPK)
  • Soil fertility
  • Organic farming
  • Environmental impact
  • Sustainable agriculture

Materials Required

  • 3 small pots
  • Garden soil
  • Seeds (moong / wheat)
  • Natural fertilizer (compost)
  • Chemical fertilizer (NPK)
  • Water
  • Scale
  • Notebook

Steps

  1. Fill 3 pots with equal soil.
  2. Label them Pot A, B, C.
  3. Plant same number of seeds.
  4. Add natural fertilizer to Pot B.
  5. Add chemical fertilizer to Pot C.
  6. Water equally and observe for 15 days.

Observation

Day No Fertilizer Natural Chemical
5 3 cm 4 cm 5 cm
10 6 cm 9 cm 11 cm
15 8 cm 13 cm 15 cm

Result

  • Chemical fertilizers show faster growth.
  • Natural fertilizers produce healthier plants.
  • No fertilizer plants grow slowly.

Conclusion

Natural fertilizers improve soil health and are eco-friendly, while chemical fertilizers provide quick nutrients but may harm soil over time.
