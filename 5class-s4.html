Water Cycle in a Bag – Science Project

Back

Introduction

Water is very important for all living things on Earth. In nature, water is constantly moving from the Earth to the air and back again. This continuous movement of water is called the water cycle.

The water cycle includes processes like evaporation, condensation, and precipitation. The Water Cycle in a Bag experiment is a simple way to observe how this natural process works.

By using a plastic bag, water, and sunlight, we can see how water evaporates, forms droplets like clouds, and falls like rain. This experiment helps students understand the natural process of the water cycle in a fun and easy way.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate the water cycle using a simple experiment.

Materials Required

  • Transparent zip-lock plastic bag
  • Water
  • Blue food color (optional)
  • Marker
  • Tape
  • Sunny window

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Take a transparent zip-lock bag.
  2. Draw the sun, clouds, and water on the bag using a marker.
  3. Pour a small amount of water into the bag.
  4. Add a drop of blue food color to make the water visible (optional).
  5. Seal the bag tightly.
  6. Tape the bag to a sunny window.
  7. Leave it for a few hours or a day and observe what happens.

Observation

  • The water inside the bag evaporates when heated by sunlight.
  • Small water droplets form on the inside of the bag like clouds.
  • The droplets fall down like rain.

Result

The experiment shows the different stages of the water cycle.

Conclusion

This model helps us understand how water moves continuously between the earth and the atmosphere.
