Working Model of Rainwater Harvesting

Back

Introduction

Water is one of the most important natural resources for life. However, in many places there is a shortage of water because rainwater is often wasted by flowing away.

Rainwater harvesting is a method of collecting and storing rainwater for future use. This water can be used for gardening, cleaning, and other household purposes.

In this project, we create a simple working model to show how rainwater can be collected from rooftops and stored in a tank. This helps us understand the importance of saving water.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate how rainwater can be collected and stored for future use.

Materials Required

  • Cardboard or thermocol base
  • Small house model
  • Plastic bottle (to act as a water tank)
  • Straw or small pipe
  • Glue or tape
  • Cutter or scissors (use with adult help)
  • Blue colored water

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Make a small house model using cardboard or thermocol.
  2. Attach a sloping roof to the house.
  3. Fix a straw or small pipe at the edge of the roof to act as a pipe for rainwater.
  4. Connect the pipe to a plastic bottle or container which will act as the storage tank.
  5. Pour water on the roof to represent rain.
  6. Observe how the water flows through the pipe and collects in the tank.

Observation

When water is poured on the roof, it flows through the pipe and collects in the storage tank.

Result

Rainwater can be collected from rooftops and stored for future use.

Conclusion

This model shows that rainwater harvesting is an effective way to save water and reduce water shortage.
