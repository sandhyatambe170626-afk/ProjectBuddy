Smart Irrigation System

Back

Aim of the Project

  • To design a smart irrigation system that automatically supplies water to plants when the soil becomes dry.

Objective

  • To save water by avoiding over-irrigation.
  • To understand the use of sensors and automation.
  • To develop an agriculture-based innovation.

Need of the Project

  • Traditional irrigation wastes a lot of water.
  • Farmers often face water scarcity.
  • Manual watering is time-consuming.
  • Smart systems help in water conservation.

Scientific Principles Used

  • Soil moisture detection
  • Sensors
  • Automation
  • Basic electronics
  • Water conservation

Materials Required

  • Soil moisture sensor
  • Arduino UNO (or simple relay module)
  • Mini DC water pump
  • Relay module
  • Plastic pipe or tube
  • Battery or power supply
  • Connecting wires
  • Plant pot with soil
  • Cardboard or thermocol base
  • Water container

Block Diagram (Concept)

Soil → Moisture Sensor → Controller → Water Pump → Plants

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Fix a plant pot on a cardboard or thermocol base.
  2. Insert the soil moisture sensor into the soil.
  3. Connect the sensor to the Arduino or relay module.
  4. Connect the water pump to the relay module.
  5. Attach a pipe from the pump to the plant pot.
  6. Place the pump inside the water container.
  7. Connect the power supply.
  8. Program the Arduino or adjust relay to turn ON the pump when soil is dry.

How It Works

  • The soil moisture sensor measures the moisture level in the soil.
  • If the soil becomes dry, the sensor sends a signal to the controller.
  • The controller activates the water pump.
  • Water flows through the pipe and irrigates the plant.
  • When the soil becomes sufficiently wet, the pump automatically stops.

Result / Output

  • Water is supplied to plants only when required.
  • No unnecessary water wastage occurs.
  • Plants remain healthy with proper irrigation.
  • Fully automatic irrigation is achieved.

Applications

  • Agriculture fields
  • Gardens
  • Greenhouses
  • Farms
  • Smart villages

Advantages

  • Saves water
  • Reduces human effort
  • Automatic operation
  • Low cost and eco-friendly
  • Improves crop yield

Conclusion

The Smart Irrigation System is an effective solution for modern agriculture. It helps farmers conserve water and ensures proper irrigation by using smart technology and soil moisture sensors.
