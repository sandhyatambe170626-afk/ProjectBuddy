Urban Green Spaces and Their Importance

Back

Introduction

Urban green spaces are areas in cities that are covered with plants, trees, grass, and natural vegetation. Examples include parks, gardens, playgrounds, roadside trees, and community gardens.

In modern cities, rapid urbanization and construction have reduced natural areas. This has led to problems like air pollution, heat, and lack of fresh air. Urban green spaces help solve these problems by improving air quality, reducing temperature, and providing a healthy environment for people.

Trees and plants in green spaces absorb carbon dioxide (CO₂) and release oxygen (O₂) through the process of photosynthesis, which is essential for life on Earth.

Aim of the Project

The aim of this project is to study the importance of urban green spaces and understand how they help improve environmental quality and human health in cities.

Vision of the Project

The vision of this project is to encourage people and communities to create and protect green spaces in urban areas to build healthier, cleaner, and more sustainable cities.

Materials Required

  • Notebook and pen
  • Camera or mobile phone (optional)
  • Measuring tape (optional)
  • Chart paper for recording data
  • Map or drawing of nearby park or garden

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Visit a nearby park, garden, or green area in your locality.
  2. Observe the types of plants, trees, and grass present in that area.
  3. Note how the green space is used by people for walking, exercise, and relaxation.
  4. Observe environmental benefits such as shade, fresh air, and cooler temperature.
  5. Record your observations in a notebook or chart.
  6. Prepare a simple diagram or map of the green space showing trees, pathways, and open areas.

Scientific Reason

Plants in urban green spaces perform photosynthesis, a process in which they use sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water to produce food and oxygen.

This process helps:

  • Reduce carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere
  • Increase oxygen supply
  • Improve air quality
  • Reduce urban heat by providing shade and cooling the air

Green spaces also support biodiversity by providing habitats for birds, insects, and small animals.

Chemical Reaction (Photosynthesis)

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → Sunlight → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Explanation:

  • CO₂ – Carbon dioxide from air
  • H₂O – Water from soil
  • Sunlight – Energy source
  • C₆H₁₂O₆ – Glucose (plant food)
  • O₂ – Oxygen released into the atmosphere

This reaction helps maintain the oxygen balance in the environment.

Importance of Urban Green Spaces

  • Improve air quality
  • Reduce urban heat
  • Provide recreation areas for people
  • Support wildlife and biodiversity
  • Improve mental and physical health

Problems When Green Spaces Are Reduced

  • Increased air pollution
  • Higher temperature in cities
  • Loss of biodiversity
  • Less recreational space for people

Conclusion

Urban green spaces are essential for maintaining a healthy and balanced environment in cities. They help reduce pollution, control temperature, and provide relaxation areas for people.

Protecting and increasing green spaces will help create cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable cities for future generations.
