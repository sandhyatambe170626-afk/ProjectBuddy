Use of Satellite Technology in Weather Prediction

Back

Introduction

Weather prediction is very important for agriculture, transportation, disaster management, and daily life. In earlier times, people predicted weather by observing clouds, wind patterns, and natural signs.

Today, advanced satellite technology helps scientists forecast weather conditions more accurately. Weather satellites orbit the Earth and capture images of clouds, storms, oceans, and land.

In India, organizations like the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) use satellites such as INSAT-3D and Kalpana-1 to monitor weather conditions. These satellites help predict cyclones, rainfall, temperature changes, and natural disasters, saving thousands of lives every year.

Aim of the Project

  • To understand how satellites help in weather forecasting.
  • To explain the importance of satellite technology.
  • To show how early warnings reduce disaster damage.
  • To create awareness about scientific advancements in meteorology.

Vision of the Project

The vision of this project is to promote scientific awareness and highlight the importance of satellite technology in protecting lives and improving weather forecasting systems.

Materials Required

  • Thermocol or cardboard base
  • Small globe or printed Earth
  • Thermocol ball (for satellite)
  • Aluminum foil
  • Cardboard pieces (for solar panels)
  • Blue and black chart paper
  • Cotton (for clouds)
  • Sketch pens
  • Thread to hang the satellite
  • Labels

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Create Earth using a small globe or by painting continents on a thermocol ball.
  2. Show India clearly and label it as “Earth – Weather Monitoring”.
  3. Make a satellite by covering a thermocol ball with aluminum foil.
  4. Attach rectangular cardboard pieces as solar panels.
  5. Hang the satellite above the Earth using thread.
  6. Draw signal waves from the satellite to a small ground control station.
  7. Use cotton to show clouds and a spiral shape to represent a cyclone.
  8. Add labels such as “Cyclone Detection”, “Rainfall Forecast”, and “Early Warning System”.

Scientific Concepts

  • Remote sensing
  • Geostationary orbit
  • Electromagnetic waves
  • Weather systems (cyclones, monsoon)
  • Disaster management

Working Model Idea (Optional)

  • Add a small LED light inside the satellite.
  • Create a rotating base to show the satellite orbiting the Earth.
  • Display printed real satellite images on a chart board.

Importance of Satellite Weather Prediction

  • Provides early cyclone warnings
  • Helps predict rainfall accurately
  • Helps farmers plan crops
  • Improves aviation safety
  • Reduces damage during natural disasters

Real-Life Example

Satellites operated by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) help monitor weather conditions in India. These satellites have helped predict major cyclones in the Bay of Bengal, allowing early warnings that reduce casualties and protect communities.

FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

1. What is a weather satellite?

A weather satellite is a satellite that collects weather data from space.

2. How do satellites predict weather?

They capture images of clouds and measure temperature, humidity, and wind patterns.

3. What is geostationary orbit?

It is an orbit where a satellite stays fixed over one place on Earth while rotating with the planet.

4. Why is satellite prediction important?

It helps in disaster prevention, weather forecasting, and planning for agriculture and transportation.

5. Who manages weather satellites in India?

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) manages weather satellites in India.

Conclusion

Satellite technology has revolutionized weather forecasting by providing accurate and timely information about storms, rainfall, and climate conditions.

By using advanced satellites, scientists can protect lives, support agriculture, and reduce disaster risks. Weather satellites are an important achievement of modern science.
