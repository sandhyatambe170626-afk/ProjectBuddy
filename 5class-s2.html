Magic Color Changing Flower – Science Project

Back

Introduction

Plants need water to grow and stay healthy. They absorb water through their roots and transport it to different parts of the plant such as the stem, leaves, and flowers. This movement of water happens through tiny tubes inside the plant.

In the Magic Color Changing Flower experiment, a white flower is placed in colored water. After some time, the petals of the flower start changing color. This happens because the flower absorbs the colored water through its stem.

This experiment helps us understand how plants take in water and move it to different parts.

Aim of the Project

To show how flowers absorb colored water through their stems.

Materials Required

  • 1 white flower (like a rose or carnation)
  • 1 transparent glass or jar
  • Water
  • Food coloring (red, blue, or any color)
  • Spoon
  • Knife or scissors (use with adult help)

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Fill the glass with water.
  2. Add a few drops of food coloring and mix well.
  3. Cut the stem of the white flower slightly at the bottom.
  4. Place the flower in the colored water.
  5. Leave it for a few hours or overnight.
  6. Slowly the petals of the flower will start changing color.

Observation

The white flower petals slowly change to the color of the water (red, blue, etc.).

Result

The flower absorbs colored water through its stem, and the color reaches the petals.

Conclusion

This experiment shows how plants absorb water from the soil and transport it to different parts of the plant.
