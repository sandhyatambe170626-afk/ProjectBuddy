Solar Water Heater – Working Model

Back

Introduction

A solar water heater is a device that uses energy from the Sun to heat water. It is an eco-friendly system that saves electricity and fuel. Solar energy is a renewable source of energy, which means it will not get finished. Solar water heaters are commonly used in homes, hotels, and hospitals to heat water for daily use. This project helps us understand how sunlight can be converted into heat energy to warm water.

Aim

To make a working model of a solar water heater and understand how solar energy is used to heat water.

Materials Required

  • Cardboard box or thermocol box
  • Black chart paper or black paint
  • Plastic or copper pipe
  • Transparent plastic sheet or glass sheet
  • Small water container or bottle
  • Tape or glue
  • Scissors
  • Water

Procedure

  1. Take a cardboard box and place black chart paper inside it.
  2. Fix a plastic or copper pipe in a zigzag shape inside the box.
  3. Connect one end of the pipe to a water container.
  4. Cover the top of the box with a transparent plastic sheet or glass so sunlight can enter.
  5. Fill the pipe with water.
  6. Keep the model in direct sunlight.
  7. After some time, the water coming out from the pipe will become warm.

Observation

  • When the model is kept in direct sunlight, the black surface absorbs heat from the Sun.
  • The pipe becomes warm after some time.
  • The water flowing through the pipe slowly increases in temperature.
  • The transparent sheet helps trap heat inside the box, making the water warmer.
  • After some time, the water coming out of the pipe is warmer than the water poured in.

Result

The experiment shows that solar energy can be used to heat water. The black surface absorbs sunlight and converts it into heat energy, which warms the water inside the pipe. Therefore, a solar water heater works effectively using sunlight without electricity.

Conclusion

The solar water heater is an efficient and eco-friendly way to heat water using sunlight. This project shows how renewable energy can be used to reduce electricity consumption and protect the environment.
