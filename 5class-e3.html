Impact of Global Warming on Polar Animals

Back

Introduction

Global warming is the gradual increase in the Earth's temperature caused by greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane. These gases trap heat in the atmosphere and increase the planet's temperature.

Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial pollution are the main causes of global warming. Polar regions like the Arctic and Antarctica are the most affected areas.

Melting ice and rising temperatures are destroying the natural habitats of polar animals such as polar bears, emperor penguins, Arctic foxes, and walruses.

This project explains how global warming affects polar animals and highlights the importance of protecting our environment.

Objective

  • To understand the causes of global warming.
  • To study the impact of climate change on polar regions.
  • To examine how melting ice affects polar animals.
  • To suggest solutions to reduce global warming.

Concepts Used

  • Greenhouse Effect
  • Climate Change
  • Melting Glaciers and Sea Ice Loss
  • Habitat Destruction
  • Food Chain Disruption
  • Environmental Conservation

Materials Required

  • Thermocol or cardboard base
  • Cotton (to represent snow)
  • Blue chart paper (for ocean)
  • Clay or plastic animal models
  • Small LED light (for sun effect)
  • Transparent plastic sheet (for ice)
  • Water spray bottle
  • Labels and charts

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Divide the base into two sections: before global warming and after global warming.
  2. Create glaciers using thermocol pieces and cover them with cotton to represent snow.
  3. Use a transparent plastic sheet to show ice.
  4. Place polar animals like polar bears and penguins on the ice.
  5. On the second side, show broken ice pieces and animals struggling.
  6. Spray small drops of water to represent melting ice.
  7. Add labels such as "Melting Ice", "Loss of Habitat", and "Rising Sea Levels".
  8. Attach a small explanation chart about global warming effects.

How It Works

Global warming increases the temperature of the Earth, which causes glaciers and polar ice to melt.

  • Melting ice destroys the natural habitat of polar animals.
  • Animals like polar bears lose the ice they use to hunt seals.
  • Penguins lose their breeding areas due to melting sea ice.
  • The food chain gets disturbed, affecting many species.

Expected Result

  • The model clearly shows the difference between a healthy polar environment and a melting polar environment.
  • Students can understand how rising temperatures affect wildlife.
  • The project spreads awareness about protecting polar ecosystems.

Conclusion

Global warming is a serious environmental issue affecting ecosystems around the world, especially in polar regions. The melting of ice threatens the survival of many animals.

Reducing pollution, saving energy, planting trees, and using renewable energy can help slow down global warming and protect polar wildlife.
