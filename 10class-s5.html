Working Model of Waste Segregation System

Back

Aim

To design and demonstrate a working model of an automatic waste segregation system that separates wet and dry waste using sensors, helping in proper waste management and supporting the Swachh Bharat Mission.

Objective

  • To automatically identify and separate wet and dry waste.
  • To reduce manual handling of garbage.
  • To spread awareness about cleanliness and recycling.
  • To promote environment-friendly waste management.

Need of the Project

  • Mixed waste causes pollution and health problems.
  • Manual segregation is unsafe and time-consuming.
  • Proper segregation helps in recycling and composting.
  • Supports government cleanliness initiatives.

Concepts Used

  • Environmental Science
  • Sensors and Automation
  • Basic Electronics
  • Waste Management

Materials Required

For Structure
  • Cardboard / Thermocol / Wooden base
  • Waste bins (2 small boxes or cups – Wet & Dry)
  • Plastic pipe or funnel (for waste input)
Electronic Components
  • Arduino UNO (optional for advanced model)
  • Moisture Sensor
  • Servo Motor
  • Jumper wires
  • Breadboard
  • Battery / Power supply
Waste Samples
  • Wet waste: fruit peels, wet paper
  • Dry waste: plastic, paper, wrappers

Principle of Working

The system works on the principle that wet waste contains moisture while dry waste does not. A moisture sensor detects the moisture level and sends a signal to the controller, which then directs the waste into the correct bin.

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Prepare the Base
    • Take a cardboard or thermocol base.
    • Fix two bins labeled Wet Waste and Dry Waste.
  2. Create Waste Input Section
    • Make a funnel or pipe at the top where waste is dropped.
  3. Fix the Moisture Sensor
    • Place the moisture sensor below the input pipe.
  4. Connect Electronics
    • Connect moisture sensor to Arduino.
    • Attach servo motor to direct waste.
  5. Programming (Optional)
    • If moisture detected → waste goes to Wet Waste bin.
    • If no moisture → waste goes to Dry Waste bin.
  6. Testing
    • Drop wet waste → goes to wet bin.
    • Drop dry waste → goes to dry bin.

Working Explanation

  • Waste is dropped into the input pipe.
  • Moisture sensor checks the moisture level.
  • Arduino processes the sensor data.
  • Servo motor rotates accordingly.
  • Waste falls into the correct bin automatically.

Result

  • Wet and dry waste are successfully separated.
  • No manual effort required.
  • The system works efficiently and accurately.

Advantages

  • Reduces human contact with waste.
  • Saves time and effort.
  • Encourages recycling.
  • Environment-friendly system.
  • Suitable for homes, schools, and public places.

Applications

  • Smart cities
  • Schools and colleges
  • Railway stations
  • Public dustbins
  • Residential societies

Conclusion

This working model of a Waste Segregation System demonstrates how technology can help solve environmental problems. By automatically separating wet and dry waste, it promotes cleanliness, recycling, and supports a sustainable future.
