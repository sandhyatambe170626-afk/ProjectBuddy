Introduction

A neighbourhood is the area around our home where people live and share common facilities such as roads, parks, schools, and markets.

Conducting a neighbourhood survey helps us understand the condition of our surroundings, including cleanliness, waste management, and public services.

This project encourages students to observe their surroundings and learn how community participation helps maintain a clean and healthy environment.

Objective

  • To study the facilities available in the neighbourhood.
  • To understand the importance of cleanliness and sanitation.
  • To collect information about waste management and public services.
  • To create awareness about keeping the neighbourhood clean.

Concepts Used

  • Community and Civic Sense
  • Cleanliness and Sanitation
  • Public Services
  • Waste Management
  • Environmental Awareness

Materials Required

  • Notebook and pen (for survey questions)
  • Chart paper or cardboard
  • Scale and pencil
  • Colors or sketch pens
  • Printed survey table (optional)
  • Pictures (optional)

Steps to Do the Survey

  1. Prepare simple survey questions such as: Are there dustbins in the area? Is garbage collected daily? Are the roads clean? Are there parks and trees nearby? Is there a proper drainage system?
  2. Visit your neighbourhood and politely ask 5–10 neighbours these questions.
  3. Record the answers in a simple table showing “Yes” or “No”.
  4. Draw a neighbourhood map showing houses, roads, parks, shops, schools, temples, and dustbins.
  5. Write a conclusion explaining the cleanliness and facilities in your neighbourhood and suggest improvements if needed.

How It Works

A survey is a method used to collect information from people by asking questions.

  • By analyzing survey answers, we can understand the problems and facilities in our neighbourhood.
  • Clean surroundings help reduce diseases and improve health.
  • Proper waste management keeps the environment clean.
  • Parks and trees improve air quality and make the area healthier.

Expected Result

  • The survey provides information about the condition of the neighbourhood.
  • Students learn the importance of cleanliness and civic responsibility.
  • The project encourages people to keep their surroundings clean and healthy.

Conclusion

A clean and well-managed neighbourhood improves the quality of life for everyone. Proper waste disposal, regular garbage collection, and green spaces help maintain a healthy environment.

Community cooperation and responsible behaviour are important for keeping neighbourhoods safe, clean, and pleasant to live in.
