Solar Powered House Model

Introduction

Electricity is very important in our daily life, but many sources of electricity can cause pollution. Solar energy is a clean and renewable source of energy that comes from the Sun.

Solar panels can capture sunlight and convert it into electricity. A solar powered house uses solar panels to produce electricity for lights and small devices.

This project helps students understand how solar energy can be used to power a house in an eco-friendly way.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate how solar energy can be used to generate electricity for a house.

Materials Required

  • Cardboard or thermocol for making the house
  • Small solar panel
  • LED light
  • Connecting wires
  • Glue or tape
  • Scissors (use with adult help)
  • Small switch (optional)

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Build a small house model using cardboard or thermocol.
  2. Fix the solar panel on the roof of the house.
  3. Connect the solar panel to the LED light using wires.
  4. Place the LED light inside the house.
  5. Keep the model in sunlight.
  6. When sunlight falls on the solar panel, it generates electricity and the LED light turns on.

Observation

When the solar panel is placed in sunlight, the LED light inside the house glows.

Result

The solar panel converts sunlight into electricity to power the light in the house.

Conclusion

This experiment shows that solar energy can be used to produce electricity for homes. Solar energy is a clean, renewable, and eco-friendly source of energy that helps reduce pollution.
