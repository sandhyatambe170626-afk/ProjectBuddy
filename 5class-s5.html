Natural Indicator – Science Project

Back

Introduction

In our daily life we use many substances like lemon juice, soap, baking soda, and vinegar. Some of them are acids and some are bases. To identify whether a substance is acidic or basic, scientists use substances called indicators.

An indicator is a substance that changes its color when added to an acid or a base. Instead of chemical indicators, we can also use natural materials such as turmeric and China rose (hibiscus) petals. These substances are known as natural indicators.

Aim of the Project

To study how natural substances like turmeric and China rose petals can be used to identify acids and bases.

Materials Required

  • Turmeric powder
  • China rose (Hibiscus) petals
  • Warm water
  • 4 small cups
  • Lemon juice
  • Vinegar
  • Baking soda solution
  • Soap solution
  • Spoon
  • White paper (for turmeric test)

Step-by-Step Procedure

A) Turmeric Indicator Test

  1. Mix turmeric powder with a little water to make a paste.
  2. Apply the paste on white paper and let it dry.
  3. Cut the paper into small strips.
  4. Put a few drops of lemon juice on one strip.
  5. Put a few drops of soap solution on another strip.
  6. Observe the color change.

B) China Rose Indicator Test

  1. Take some China rose petals.
  2. Crush them in warm water.
  3. Filter the liquid into a cup.
  4. Add a few drops of lemon juice, vinegar, baking soda solution, and soap solution in separate cups.
  5. Observe the color changes.

Observation

Turmeric Indicator

  • No color change with acids.
  • Turns red or reddish-brown with bases.

China Rose Indicator

  • Turns dark pink or red in acids.
  • Turns green in bases.

Result

Different substances cause different color changes in natural indicators, helping us identify acids and bases.

Conclusion

Natural substances like turmeric and China rose petals can be used as indicators to identify acids and bases. These indicators are safe, eco-friendly, and easily available, making them a simple and inexpensive option for science experiments.
