Smart Automatic Fire Detection & Alarm System

Back

Aim

To design and demonstrate a Smart Automatic Fire Detection & Alarm System that detects fire (heat or smoke) and automatically triggers an alarm to ensure safety.

Objective

  • To understand the working of fire detection systems.
  • To learn about chemical reactions and electric circuits.
  • To demonstrate how safety systems help prevent accidents in homes, schools, and factories.

Concepts Used

  • Chemical reactions – smoke is formed due to combustion.
  • Heat and temperature detection.
  • Electric circuits, buzzer, and battery.
  • Early warning safety systems.

Materials Required

No Materials
1Arduino UNO
2Flame sensor / Smoke sensor (MQ-2)
3Buzzer
4Red LED light
5Resistors
6Breadboard
7Connecting wires
89V Battery or power supply
9Cardboard for model house
10Glue, scissors, chart paper

Steps to Make the Model

  1. Prepare the Base
    • Create a small cardboard house model.
    • Fix it on a strong base board.
  2. Setup the Circuit
    • Connect the smoke or flame sensor to Arduino.
    • Connect buzzer and LED to output pins.
    • Attach battery to power the circuit.
  3. Programming (if using Arduino)
    • Upload a program that detects smoke.
    • The buzzer and LED turn ON when fire is detected.
  4. Fix the Components
    • Place sensor inside the model house.
    • Place buzzer and LED outside the house.
  5. Testing
    • Bring an incense stick (agarbatti) near the sensor.
    • When smoke is detected, alarm should ring.

How It Works

  • Fire produces smoke and heat.
  • The smoke sensor detects harmful gases.
  • The sensor sends signal to the Arduino.
  • Arduino activates buzzer and LED.
  • The alarm alerts people about the fire.

Result

  • The system detects smoke automatically.
  • The alarm activates instantly.
  • This demonstrates real fire alarm systems used in buildings.

Applications

  • Homes
  • Schools
  • Factories
  • Offices
  • Shopping malls
  • Hospitals

Advantages

  • Early fire detection
  • Prevents major accidents
  • Easy installation
  • Low-cost safety system

Conclusion

The Smart Automatic Fire Detection & Alarm System helps protect life and property by providing early fire warnings. This project demonstrates how technology can improve safety in homes and buildings.
